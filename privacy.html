Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026
Designed for children · COPPA & UK GDPR compliant

Who we are

Crescendo is a music practice tracker for children. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a parent or guardian.

What data we collect

Stored only on your device — never sent to us:

  • Your child's name and chosen avatar
  • Parent or guardian name
  • Practice session logs (instrument, duration, date)
  • Stamps earned, XP, and streak count

We never collect your child's email address, precise location, photos, device advertising identifiers, or any information that identifies them personally outside of this device.

How we use data

All data stays on your device. We do not transmit, sell, or share any personal information with third parties or advertisers.

Children's privacy (COPPA & UK GDPR)

Crescendo is designed for children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR). We do not collect personal information from children without verifiable parental consent obtained during app setup.

Third-party services

Crescendo uses no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The app makes no external network requests during normal use.

Data storage & deletion

All personal data is stored locally on your device using app local storage. It is never uploaded to our servers. You can permanently delete all data at any time using the Reset progress button inside the app.

Your rights

As a parent or guardian you may:

  • Review the data stored by using the app
  • Delete all data at any time via Reset progress
  • Withdraw consent by uninstalling the app

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance.
